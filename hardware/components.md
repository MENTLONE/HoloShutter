# Bill of materials

This list describes the **working prototype configuration**, not a packaged kit.

| Qty | Part | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Arduino Nano RP2040 Connect | Built-in Wi-Fi; Arduino Mbed OS Nano board support |
| 1 | SG90 micro servo | Match shaft and mounting geometry to your shutter |
| 1 | Regulated 5 V servo power supply | Size for servo startup/stall current; avoid powering from GPIO |
| 1 | Shutter blade | Opaque at the laser wavelength; mechanically secure |
| 1 | Servo/shutter mounting bracket | Can be fabricated or 3D printed |
| Several | Jumper wires / connectors | Secure connections preferred over loose breadboard wires |
| 1 | USB cable | Programming and Nano power |
| Optional | Enclosure, fasteners, strain relief | Recommended for repeatable use |

**Not yet specified:** Exact power supply model, servo mount STL, shutter blade dimensions, fasteners, and enclosure. These will be documented after measurement rather than guessed.
